# Multivend Services Ltd — Company profile and machine fleet

## Company
- Name: Multivend Services Limited (Malta). ISO certified.
- Founded in 1997 by Mr. Adrian Gomez Blanco (Founder & Director), a former vending machine technician.
- Over thirty employees; equipment installed across Malta and Gozo. Operates the majority of the vending sector in the Maltese Islands.

## Services
- Vending machines: snack, cold drinks and hot drinks (coffee/tea) for small, medium and large organizations, with day-to-day vending operation.
- HoReCa coffee machines (Hotels, Restaurants, Catering): bean-to-cup espresso, fresh milk, fresh-brew / french press, espresso point, pods, capsules and instant solutions.
- Technical Department: maintenance contracts and one-time servicing, repairs and complete overhauls, spare parts supply (from circuit boards to coffee filters), for all types of vending machines and HoReCa coffee machines.
