================ 11. Técnico real ================

👤 I'm a Multivend tech on site. Opera Touch shows grinder lock, what are the likely causes and what do I check?
🤖 [7s src=EN_OperaTouch_Manual,EN_Kometa_Manual,Necta_OperaTouch_brochure,EN_KreaTouch_Manual]
On Opera Touch, “grinder lock” means the machine is not detecting grinder rotation during grinding. Manual says likely blockage/foreign body; espresso-based selections are disabled. 

Check in this order:

1. Safety first
Switch off at the wall before touching the grinder area. If opening the door: door cuts power; do visual checks with door open, powered tests only after closing. Service key only if needed and you’re authorised.

2. Bean hopper / outlet
Check hopper is seated, beans are flowing, no bridge/clump at the outlet.

3. Grinder jam
Look for foreign body, stone, screw, hard bean, compacted coffee at grinder inlet/outlet. Clear only with power OFF.

4. Grinder movement
After clearing, close door and try an espresso selection/reset. Listen if grinder starts or only hums.

5. If it hums or re-locks
Suspect grinder blocked mechanically, motor/capacitor/wiring issue, or rotation sensor not seeing movement.

Also note: “no coffee” is different — that’s when ground dose is not reached in the doser within 15 seconds. Grinder lock is specifically rotation not detected. 

Source: Necta Opera Touch manual.
